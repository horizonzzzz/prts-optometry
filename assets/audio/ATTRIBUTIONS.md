# Battle sound effects

The `battle-*.wav` files were converted from Kenney's CC0 **Sci-Fi Sounds** pack:

- Source: <https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds>
- Download: <https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/sci-fi-sounds/6b296f9ecf-1677589334/kenney_sci-fi-sounds.zip>
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0), as stated in the pack's `License.txt`.
- Original sounds: `laserSmall_000`, `laserSmall_001`, `forceField_000`, `impactMetal_002`, `impactMetal_003`, `explosionCrunch_004`, `lowFrequency_explosion_001`.

The files are resampled to 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV for predictable browser decoding.
